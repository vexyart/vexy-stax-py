## Goal

- I want to have a CLI and the renderer
- The app accepts a JSON "scene" saved by the JS tool (for example @test-img/layer123.json ) 
- The app can export an image (at @1, @2, @4 sizes)
- The app can export a @1, @2, @4 size MOV/MP4 of the hero shot animation that starts with the JSON scene, animates towards a flat full front view of the last slide (and then the distance between the slides is reduced to 0), then the camera moves back to the original position. The fps, length of the move, and length of the hold at the front view are all configurable.
- There may be some additional options to control the rendering quality, but overall we want a very high-quality render. The slides should have some metalic or glossy or matte appearance, they should reflect nicely, cast slight shadows. They should look like the actual slides, with full saturation. The background color and background opacity are configurable.
